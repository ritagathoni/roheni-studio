## Known limitations of the first version

Collection II currently shows supplied digital concepts and design studies, rather than a launched collection or photographic proof of completed garments. Image credits and rights need confirmation. No final application PDF is included. Content is rendered with JavaScript and fonts are externally loaded. There is no form backend, shopping cart, tracking or newsletter subscription.
