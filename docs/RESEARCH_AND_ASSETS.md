# Research, sources and asset treatment

## Font review

- Plus Jakarta Sans, Tokotype: https://github.com/tokotype/PlusJakartaSans — selected for body/navigation.
- League Gothic, The League of Moveable Type: https://www.theleagueofmoveabletype.com/league-gothic — selected for narrow accents.
- Fraunces: https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Fraunces — retained for editorial continuity.
- Collletttivo catalogue: https://www.collletttivo.it/typefaces — open-source catalogue reviewed; Ortica considered as a more literary alternative. Its detail page timed out in retrieval.
- Velvetyne Pilowlava: https://velvetyne.fr/fonts/pilowlava/ — current foundry page redirected to its strike notice. The designers’ GitLab README identifies the font and OFL. Its decorative weight would compete with the original logo, so it was not added.
- Nexover on Befonts: https://befonts.com/nexover-font.html — the listing explicitly restricts the demo to personal use. No demo files were downloaded or embedded.
- Earlier Free Faces and Uncut exploration informed the refinement; Barriecito, Archivo and the unused commercial family names are superseded by the three-family system in premium.css.

## Software review

Figma/FigJam offers moodboard tools: https://www.figma.com/templates/moodboard-maker/ . Canva provides a free video editor: https://www.canva.com/video-editor/ . Affinity describes unified vector/pixel/layout tools and Cavalry animation integration: https://www.affinity.studio/ . Blender provides free 3D/animation tools: https://www.blender.org/features/ . Photopea supports layer-based animations: https://www.photopea.com/learn/animations .

This edition uses native website interactions and FFmpeg for its short material film. It does not require access to the owner’s Canva account or installation of those design apps. No claim is made that a Figma, Canva, Affinity, Blender or Photopea project was created.
