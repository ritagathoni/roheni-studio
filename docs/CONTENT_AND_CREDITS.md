# Content and credit record

## Editorial position

The copy follows the story developed with Rita: overlooked materials can become extraordinary clothes; reducing pollution around Gikomba is a goal; the work promotes Kenyan making and African artistry; visitors can encounter that artistry through clothing.

Do not turn an intention into a measured result. The site does not claim a verified waste-diversion tonnage, carbon reduction, zero-waste production system or operational take-back programme.

## Statements to verify before public release

- More than KSh500,000 in cumulative sales after one debut collection: confirm dates and accounting basis.
- United Nations recognition during zero-waste activities in 2025: add exact event and form of recognition; do not present it as a prize or endorsement unless supported.
- Tony Elumelu Foundation grant support: confirm the preferred public description.
- Fairmont collaborations: describe the actual relationship and avoid implying a formal retail partnership unless confirmed.
- BAF recognition: add the exact Building Africa Fashion post and date.
- Creator roles: supplied documents name Rita and Seth differently. Confirm personal design authorship, company roles and collaborators per project.

## Application use

Verify the live programme’s ownership, age, portfolio and submission rules. A public portfolio and a grant application have different requirements. Do not alter credit wording to imply sole ownership if work is shared. Prepare the final visual PDF separately; do not submit this internal documentation as the creative portfolio.
